About us

Welcome to Museum of Consoles, your digital archive dedicated to the history, hardware, and nostalgia of video game systems.
Our Core Focus:
Hardware Deep Dives: Specs, engineering, and design stories behind iconic systems.
Preservation & Retrospectives: Documenting system lifecycles, launch games, and gaming culture.
Obscure & Rare Gaming: Spotlighting forgotten flops, rare prototypes, and regional variants.
